The Problem

Why KisanMitra?

⏳

Hours of Waiting

Farmers spend 6-8 hours waiting in unorganized queues at procurement centres, losing a full day of productive work.

📋

No Transparency

Paper-based systems leave farmers with zero visibility into queue position, weighment records, or payment timelines.

🚜

Congestion at Mandis

Hundreds of farmers arrive simultaneously causing chaotic congestion, vehicle blockages, and crop spoilage.

💰

Delayed Payments

Lack of digital tracking means farmers have no way to know when their procurement payment will be processed.

The Solution

How KisanMitra Works

A seamless 9-step digital journey that transforms the procurement experience

1
📱

Farmer Registration

Simple mobile/Aadhaar-linked sign-up process. Farmers create accounts using their phone number and verify via OTP.

2
📅

Slot Booking

Choose a preferred procurement centre, date, and time slot. The system shows real-time slot availability to prevent overcrowding.

3
🔔

SMS / App Notification

Instant confirmation via SMS and in-app notification with slot details, directions, and a reminder before the appointment.

4
📊

Real-time Queue Management

A digital token system with live position tracking. Farmers can see exactly how many people are ahead in the queue.

5
🔊

Turn Approaching Alert

Automatic notification when the farmer's turn is approaching, so they don't need to wait physically at the centre.

6
✅

Arrival & Quality Check

Staff verifies crop quality upon arrival. Results are digitally recorded with photos and grading for full transparency.

7
⚖️

Weighment

Quantity is recorded digitally via IoT-enabled weighing systems. Farmers receive instant weight confirmation on their device.

8
📡

Procurement Status Update

Live tracking of procurement status in the app — from quality check to weighment to payment initiation.

9
💳

Payment Processed

Direct bank transfer with SMS/app notification. Complete payment history and digital receipts accessible anytime.

🎯

Result: Reduced Congestion & Waiting Time

Farmers save hours, mandis run efficiently, and the entire procurement process becomes transparent and dignified.
